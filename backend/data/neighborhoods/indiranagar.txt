# Indiranagar — Neighborhood Profile

## Overview

Indiranagar is an upscale residential and commercial neighborhood in eastern Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Located approximately 6 km east of the city center, it is bounded by Old Airport Road to the north, HAL 2nd Stage to the east, Domlur to the west, and Ejipura/Koramangala to the south. The area is organized around two main arterials — 100 Feet Road (officially Swami Vivekananda Road) and 12th Main Road — which serve as the commercial and social spine of the neighborhood.

## History

Indiranagar was one of the earliest planned layouts in Bengaluru, developed by the BDA in the 1960s and 1970s. It was named after India's first Prime Minister, Jawaharlal Nehru (through the association with Indira Gandhi). Originally a quiet residential area with independent houses, Indiranagar began its transformation in the late 1990s and 2000s as Bengaluru's IT boom brought a wave of young professionals seeking accommodation close to their offices on the Outer Ring Road. By 2015, 100 Feet Road had become one of Bengaluru's most fashionable commercial streets, lined with boutiques, restaurants, cafes, and pubs.

## Character & Vibe

Indiranagar is considered one of Bengaluru's most upscale and trendy neighborhoods:
- Often called the "SoHo of Bengaluru" for its blend of culture, dining, and retail
- 100 Feet Road is the social epicenter — a bustling strip of cafes, restaurants, branded stores, and boutiques
- 12th Main Road is the alternative hub — more indie, with bookstores, art galleries, and quirky cafes
- The residential lanes (1st Stage, 2nd Stage) are tree-lined, relatively quiet, and retain a garden-suburb feel
- The neighborhood has a polished, cosmopolitan atmosphere that appeals to professionals and expats
- Weekend mornings see joggers, cyclists, and dog walkers in the parks and internal roads

## Food & Dining

Indiranagar is one of Bengaluru's top dining destinations:
- Over 300 restaurants, cafes, and bars along 100 Feet Road and 12th Main
- Eclectic cuisine mix: Japanese (Harima), Korean, Mediterranean, Italian, Thai, South Indian (MTR branch), and more
- Popular cafes: Third Wave Coffee, Blue Tokai, Dyu Art Cafe (art gallery + cafe combo)
- Fine dining options alongside street food stalls
- Dessert and bakery scene: The Oberoi Patisserie, Glen's Bakehouse, Aubree
- Most restaurants cater to a premium segment — average meal cost is higher than other Bengaluru neighborhoods

## Nightlife & Entertainment

Indiranagar rivals Koramangala as Bengaluru's nightlife capital:
- Dense concentration of pubs and bars along 12th Main and 100 Feet Road
- Popular nightlife venues: Toit (original location), Social, The Permit Room, Toast & Tonic, Bob's Bar
- Live music venues and comedy clubs operate several nights a week
- Weekend evenings see heavy traffic and crowds along 100 Feet Road
- Several rooftop bars with skyline views
- The area has a more upscale nightlife vibe compared to Koramangala — cocktail bars and wine lounges

## Safety

Indiranagar is widely regarded as one of Bengaluru's safest neighborhoods:
- Affluent residential area with active resident welfare associations (RWAs)
- Well-lit streets, especially along 100 Feet Road and 12th Main
- Regular police patrols and CCTV coverage on main roads
- The residential stages (1st and 2nd Stage) are quiet and family-friendly
- Women's safety is generally considered good — the area has a reputation for being walkable at night
- Heavy foot traffic on main roads provides natural surveillance
- Proximity to Indiranagar Police Station provides quick response times
- Standard precautions apply for late-night travel in less-trafficked internal lanes

## Transit & Connectivity

Indiranagar is one of the best-connected neighborhoods in Bengaluru:
- **Metro**: Indiranagar Metro Station on the Purple Line provides direct connectivity to MG Road, Majestic, Vijayanagar, and beyond. This is a major advantage — the metro runs every 5-10 minutes during peak hours
- **Bus**: BMTC routes connect to Majestic, Whitefield, Electronic City, and Silk Board
- **Key Roads**: 100 Feet Road, Old Airport Road, CMH Road (linking to Ulsoor), HAL Old Airport Road
- **Airport**: Approximately 32-35 km from Kempegowda International Airport (1–1.5 hours via NH44)
- **Commute to IT hubs**: 25-35 min to Whitefield (Outer Ring Road), 30-40 min to Electronic City, 10-15 min to MG Road (via metro)
- **Ride-hailing**: Excellent Uber/Ola availability; auto-rickshaws plentiful but may charge premium fares

## Who Lives Here

Indiranagar has a diverse but predominantly affluent population:
- Mid-to-senior level professionals in tech, consulting, and corporate sectors (aged 28-45)
- Expats and returning NRIs — Indiranagar has one of Bengaluru's highest expat concentrations
- Young couples and small families who want an urban lifestyle with good amenities
- Artists, designers, and creative professionals drawn by the cultural scene
- Long-term Bengaluru residents in independent houses (1st Stage, 2nd Stage) — many since the original BDA allotment
- Fewer students compared to Koramangala — rents are higher

## Rent Ranges

Typical monthly rents in Indiranagar:
- 1 BHK: ₹18,000 – ₹30,000
- 2 BHK: ₹30,000 – ₹50,000
- 3 BHK: ₹50,000 – ₹80,000
- Indiranagar commands some of the highest rents in Bengaluru after central areas like MG Road
- Properties near the metro station or 100 Feet Road carry a location premium
- Fully-furnished apartments are in high demand from the expat community
