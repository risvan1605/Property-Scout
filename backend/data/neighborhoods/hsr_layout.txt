## Overview

HSR Layout (Hosur-Sarjapur Road Layout) is a well-planned residential neighborhood in southeastern Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Located approximately 12 km from the city center, it is bounded by Koramangala to the north, Bommanahalli to the south, BTM Layout to the west, and Sarjapur Road to the east. The layout is organized into sectors (Sector 1 through Sector 7), each with a planned mix of residential plots, parks, and commercial zones. HSR Layout is known for its wide roads, abundant green spaces, and strong community feel.

## History

HSR Layout was developed by the BDA in the early 1990s as a planned residential extension to accommodate Bengaluru's growing population. The name comes from its location along the Hosur-Sarjapur Road corridor. For the first decade, it was considered a far-flung suburb with limited infrastructure. The turning point came in the mid-2000s when the IT corridor along Outer Ring Road (ORR) and Sarjapur Road attracted major tech parks (like RMZ Ecoworld), bringing a massive influx of IT professionals. By 2015, HSR Layout had transformed from a sleepy suburb into one of Bengaluru's most desirable residential areas, combining affordable rents with proximity to tech workplaces.

## Character & Vibe

HSR Layout offers a balanced, livable neighborhood character:
- Known as a "startup-friendly" area — many early-stage startups and freelancers operate from HSR
- More residential and family-oriented compared to Koramangala and Indiranagar
- The wide, tree-lined roads and BDA parks give it a spacious, green feel uncommon in dense Bengaluru
- Sector 2 and Sector 7 are the commercial hubs with restaurants, cafes, and shops
- The 27th Main Road is the primary commercial strip — a growing dining and shopping destination
- Weekend farmer's markets and community events are common in HSR parks
- The vibe is "young professional" — relaxed, tech-savvy, and community-oriented

## Food & Dining

HSR Layout has rapidly become a food destination in its own right:
- 27th Main Road and Sector 2 host most of the dining options
- Strong cafe culture: Third Wave Coffee, Matteo Coffea, Blue Tokai, and numerous indie cafes
- Popular restaurants: Meghana Foods, Empire, Hole in the Wall (cafe), Smoke House Deli
- Growing number of cloud kitchens and delivery-first restaurants
- Good street food options: chaat stalls, dosa carts, and juice shops along internal roads
- More affordable dining compared to Indiranagar and Koramangala — average meal cost is 20-30% lower
- Several co-working cafes where freelancers and remote workers spend the day

## Nightlife & Entertainment

HSR Layout has a quieter nightlife scene compared to Koramangala and Indiranagar:
- Fewer pubs and bars, but the scene is growing along 27th Main Road
- Notable spots: Cycle Gap (craft beer), Plan B, and a few sports bars
- Most nightlife activity winds down by 11 PM — it's more of a "dinner out" neighborhood than a "bar hopping" one
- Weekend entertainment leans toward house parties, community events, and outdoor activities
- BDA parks (especially the Sector 2 park) are popular for evening walks, jogging, and weekend gatherings
- Better suited for residents who prefer a quieter evening lifestyle with occasional going-out options

## Safety

HSR Layout is considered one of the safest neighborhoods in Bengaluru:
- Planned BDA layout with well-organized sectors and wide roads — good visibility and vehicle access
- Active resident welfare associations in every sector — regular neighborhood watch and security patrols
- Well-lit main roads and park areas
- Lower crime rates compared to more commercial neighborhoods like Koramangala
- Strong community bonds — neighbors know each other, which provides natural social surveillance
- HSR Layout Police Station is centrally located for quick response
- Very safe for families, women, and solo residents
- Internal roads are generally quiet and low-traffic, making evening walks comfortable
- Some concerns about stray dogs in the park areas at night — but this is minor

## Transit & Connectivity

HSR Layout has improving but moderate connectivity:
- **Metro**: HSR Layout Metro Station on the Yellow Line (recently operational) connects to Silk Board and beyond. Phase 2 expansion will further improve metro access
- **Bus**: BMTC services connect to Silk Board, Majestic, Electronic City, and Whitefield
- **Key Roads**: Outer Ring Road (ORR), Hosur Road, and Sarjapur Road provide arterial connectivity
- **Silk Board Junction**: Located at the northwest corner — a critical but congested transit hub
- **Airport**: Approximately 38-42 km from Kempegowda International Airport (1.5–2.5 hours depending on traffic)
- **Commute to IT hubs**: 15-25 min to Electronic City, 10-20 min to ORR tech parks (RMZ Ecoworld, Prestige Tech Park), 40-50 min to Whitefield
- The Outer Ring Road proximity is HSR's biggest connectivity advantage — most major IT parks are along the ORR

## Who Lives Here

HSR Layout has a distinctive residential profile:
- IT professionals aged 25-35 — the dominant demographic, drawn by ORR/Sarjapur Road proximity
- Young couples and small families who want a quieter alternative to Koramangala with lower rents
- Startup founders and freelancers who work from co-working spaces or home
- Students from nearby engineering colleges and coaching centers
- Middle-class families in independent houses — many original BDA allottees from the 1990s
- Growing community of pet owners — the parks and wide roads are dog-friendly
- Fewer expats compared to Indiranagar — the community is predominantly Indian

## Rent Ranges

Typical monthly rents in HSR Layout:
- 1 BHK: ₹12,000 – ₹20,000
- 2 BHK: ₹20,000 – ₹35,000
- 3 BHK: ₹35,000 – ₹55,000
- HSR Layout offers 15-25% lower rents compared to Koramangala and Indiranagar
- New apartment complexes with modern amenities command higher rents
- Proximity to ORR and metro station affects pricing — properties closer to these transit points cost more
- Good value-for-money compared to other popular Bengaluru neighborhoods
