# Koramangala — Neighborhood Profile

## Overview

Koramangala is one of the most prominent residential and commercial neighborhoods in southeastern Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Located approximately 7 km from the city center (Majestic), it is bounded by Ejipura to the north, HSR Layout to the south, Madiwala and BTM Layout to the east, and Jayanagar to the west. The neighborhood is divided into eight blocks (1st Block through 8th Block), each with a distinct character.

## History

Originally an agrarian settlement, Koramangala was developed by the Bangalore Development Authority (BDA) in the 1970s and 1980s as a planned residential layout. The name is believed to derive from the Kannada words "Kora" (meaning a type of grass) and "Mangala" (meaning auspicious). Through the 1990s it was considered a quiet residential area. The transformation began in the early 2000s with the IT boom, as proximity to Electronic City and the Outer Ring Road tech corridors attracted young professionals. By 2010, Koramangala had emerged as Bengaluru's startup capital, with numerous tech companies choosing it as their base.

## Character & Vibe

Koramangala is known for its vibrant, youthful energy. It is often called the "startup hub" of Bengaluru, hosting offices and co-working spaces for hundreds of startups and tech companies. The 5th and 6th Blocks are the commercial heart, lined with restaurants, cafes, boutique shops, and pubs. The 1st and 3rd Blocks are more residential and quieter. The area has a cosmopolitan feel with a diverse mix of young professionals, entrepreneurs, students (IIM Bangalore is located here), and established families.

## Food & Dining

Koramangala is a food lover's paradise with some of Bengaluru's most popular restaurants and eateries:
- The area has over 200 restaurants and cafes within its blocks
- Popular cuisines: North Indian, South Indian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Continental
- Notable food streets along the 5th Block and 80 Feet Road
- Well-known establishments include Meghana Foods (biryani), Truffles (burgers), Third Wave Coffee, Toit (brewery)
- Late-night food options are abundant, with many eateries open until midnight or later

## Nightlife & Entertainment

Koramangala has one of the most active nightlife scenes in Bengaluru:
- Numerous pubs, bars, and microbreweries concentrated in 5th Block and along 80 Feet Road
- Popular venues include Toit, The Biere Club, Arbor Brewing Company, and Communiti
- Several gaming lounges and entertainment zones
- Weekend events, live music, and comedy shows are common
- The area sees significant foot traffic on weekend evenings

## Safety

Koramangala is generally considered a safe neighborhood:
- Active residential community with neighborhood watch programs in the residential blocks
- Well-lit main roads (80 Feet Road, 100 Feet Road) with regular police patrols
- The 1st and 3rd Blocks are quieter and considered particularly safe for families
- The commercial areas (5th, 6th Block) can be crowded and noisy, especially on weekends
- Auto-rickshaw and cab availability is excellent, reducing the need for walking late at night
- Some concerns about petty theft in crowded commercial areas — standard urban precautions recommended
- Multiple ATMs, pharmacies, and convenience stores operate 24/7

## Transit & Connectivity

Koramangala has good connectivity to the rest of Bengaluru:
- **Metro**: The nearest metro stations are currently Lakkasandra and HSR Layout on the Yellow Line (under construction / recently opened). The Koramangala area is expected to be better served once Namma Metro Phase 2 is fully operational
- **Bus**: BMTC bus services connect Koramangala to Majestic, Silk Board, Electronic City, and Whitefield via multiple routes
- **Key Roads**: 80 Feet Road, 100 Feet Road, Hosur Road, and the Outer Ring Road provide arterial connectivity
- **Silk Board Junction**: Located nearby — a major transit hub (though infamous for traffic congestion)
- **Airport**: Approximately 35-40 km from Kempegowda International Airport (1.5–2 hours depending on traffic)
- **Commute to IT hubs**: 20-30 min to Electronic City, 40-50 min to Whitefield, 15-20 min to MG Road

## Who Lives Here

Koramangala attracts a specific demographic:
- Young professionals aged 22-35 working in tech, startups, and corporate sectors
- Startup founders and entrepreneurs — many startups are headquartered here
- Students from nearby IIM Bangalore, Christ University, and other institutions
- Working couples without children who value proximity to dining, nightlife, and workplaces
- Established families in the quieter 1st and 3rd Blocks — many long-term residents from the BDA layout era
- A growing community of freelancers and remote workers using co-working spaces

## Rent Ranges

Typical monthly rents in Koramangala:
- 1 BHK: ₹15,000 – ₹25,000
- 2 BHK: ₹25,000 – ₹40,000
- 3 BHK: ₹40,000 – ₹65,000
- Rents are among the highest in Bengaluru due to demand from the tech workforce
- Fully-furnished apartments command a 30-50% premium over unfurnished
